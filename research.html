Research

Technology-enhanced mathematics education, grounded in culture and measurement.

Research spans mathematics education, Rasch measurement, technology-enhanced learning, GeoGebra, teacher education, numeracy, and ethnomathematics — with a current focus on integrating AI into GeoGebra-supported learning trajectories.

§01 — Focus

Three threads of inquiry

i

GeoGebra & AI-supported learning

Designing and validating learning trajectories that combine GeoGebra applets with AI assistance (e.g. ChatGPT) to support conceptual understanding of statistical graphs and numeracy.
ii

Rasch & Many-Facet measurement

Applying Rasch Rating Scale and Many-Facet Rasch Models to validate instruments, assess bias, and measure change in student reasoning and self-regulated learning.
iii

Ethnomathematics & culture

Embedding local culture — traditional food, folklore, and cultural heritage sites — into statistical reasoning and mathematics learning through the Ethno-Flipped Classroom model.

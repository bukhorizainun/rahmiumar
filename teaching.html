Teaching

Teaching mathematics, statistics, and the craft of research.

Since 2015, teaching and supervision at Universitas Potensi Utama have spanned undergraduate and postgraduate levels, with a focus on student-centred learning and research training.

§01 — Practice

How teaching is approached

i

Courses taught

Undergraduate and postgraduate courses in mathematics, statistics, and research methodology at Universitas Potensi Utama.
ii

Learning design

Development of teaching materials and student-centred learning strategies, including the Ethno-Flipped Classroom model and GeoGebra-based activities.
iii

Student supervision

Supervising undergraduate and postgraduate students in research projects, theses, and academic publications.
